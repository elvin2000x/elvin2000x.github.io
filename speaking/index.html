The talks

The core talk · any audience

Get Real Work Out of AI

Why two people get different answers from the same AI tool, and the three-part brief that gets you the better one. Then how to fix a weak draft with one follow-up, and how to catch AI's confident mistakes before you repeat them. Every step is done live on a real task, so people see the prompt, the answer and the fix.

People leave with: the Brief card, the Fix card and the Check card, one page each.

The AI Skills Employers Now Expect

The AI habits that help in a job search and on the job, practised on real tasks: tailoring a resume to a posting, preparing for an interview, writing a follow-up. Includes what never to paste into an AI tool, and how to describe AI skills in an interview without overselling them.

About an hour

Which parts of the work in a small business AI can take on today, which parts stay with the owner, and how to keep client information safe while you try it. Worked live on everyday tasks like a customer email, a quote follow-up and a policy summary.

What a talk looks like

  • Live demos on everyday tasks, so the room sees each prompt, the answer it gets, and how to fix it.
  • Nobody needs a laptop or a paid account. Anyone with a phone can try the prompts in a free AI tool as we go.
  • Time for questions at the end, and I stay after for anyone who wants to ask one in person.
  • Everyone leaves with the one-page cards from the talk, free, with no sign-up needed.
  • In person anywhere in the GTA that transit reaches, or virtual on your platform.

07

Questions hosts ask

What do you need from us?

A screen or projector with an HDMI input, and a rough headcount. Wi-Fi helps for the live demos, and I bring a backup in case the connection drops.

Can you tailor the talk to our group?

Yes. Tell me who will be there and what they struggle with, and I'll build the live examples around their work.

How far ahead should we book?

Four to six weeks is comfortable. Shorter notice can work if a date is open, so ask.

We're a business or a paid event. Can you still speak?

Send the form and tell me about the event, and I'll reply with what's possible.
