The problem

The tool is not the bottleneck. The setup is.

Almost every "AI did not work for us" story is the same story. Somebody bought a licence, opened a blank chat, and had to explain their business before every single task. Nothing carried over from yesterday. The output was generic because the input was generic. After three weeks it went unused, and the conclusion was that the tool was overhyped.

The people getting real value did one thing differently. They put their context somewhere the tool reads every time, built the jobs they repeat into something reusable, and connected it to the systems where their work already lives. It is not complicated. It is just fiddly, and it never quite becomes this week's priority.

That is the entire job I am offering to do.

A place your AI tool already knows your business: a project holding your real context and documents, skills built for the jobs you repeat, connectors reaching the tools you already use, and templates written for your actual deliverables. Instead of explaining yourself from scratch every session, you open it and it already knows.
