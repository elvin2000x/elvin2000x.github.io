Frequently Asked Questions

Do you only work with businesses in these 19 cities?

No. These are the cities I built dedicated pages for, and there is a page for every province as well, but I work with businesses anywhere in Canada, remotely. If your city is not listed, the same three services and the same pricing still apply. Get in touch and I will tell you honestly whether it is a fit.

Is pricing different depending on my city?

No. Pricing is the same everywhere in Canada: Google Ads Management is $1,500 a month plus your own ad spend, SEO Consulting is $1,200 a month, and AIO Consulting is a one-time fixed-scope build for $1,800. Nothing changes based on location.

Are you actually based in Toronto?

Yes, genuinely. I am not claiming offices in any of these cities. I work from Toronto and deliver every engagement remotely, over video, email, and inside your own accounts, regardless of where your business is located.

It doesn't change anything. Send a note about your business and what you're trying to fix, and I'll tell you honestly which service is the right starting point.
