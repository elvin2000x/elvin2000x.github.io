What is actually different about marketing in Calgary

Calgary carries 221 corporate head offices employing nearly 30,000 people, which is remarkable for a metro of 1.5 million and it changes who your customer is. A large share of Calgary business is B2B, sold into decision makers at head offices on long cycles, with procurement and committees in the way. Nobody signs a six figure contract off a search ad. What search does for you there is make you findable and credible while somebody quietly builds a shortlist, which is SEO and AIO work rather than paid.

The other half of the city is an ordinary consumer economy of trades, clinics, restaurants and home services, where Google Ads works exactly the way it works anywhere. Those two Calgary businesses need almost opposite plans, and the first thing worth establishing is which one you are.

Then there is the cycle. Calgary budgets move with energy prices in a way that has nothing to do with whether your campaigns are working, and the businesses that come through a downturn intact are the ones with a channel that keeps producing when the ad budget gets cut. That is the strongest practical argument for Calgary businesses to build SEO alongside ads rather than after them.

Questions Calgary businesses ask

Do you have an office in Calgary?

No. I am based in Toronto and work from home, so there is no Calgary office and I am not going to imply there is. Everything is run remotely over video and email, which is how I run every account regardless of city.

We sell B2B into head offices. Will Google Ads help?

Some, and less than you would hope. Ads can capture the small number of people actively searching for what you sell, but most of your buying cycle happens before anyone types a commercial keyword. Where search earns its money for a Calgary B2B business is credibility during research, which is SEO and AIO work. I would rather tell you that before you fund a campaign than after.

What happens to our marketing if the economy turns?

Ads stop producing the day you stop paying, which is exactly when budgets get cut. That is not an argument against ads, it is an argument for having rankings and AI visibility built before you need them. If you can only afford one channel and you are worried about the cycle, I would build the one that keeps working when the spending stops.

Which service should a Calgary business start with?

If your customer is a household with an urgent problem, Google Ads. If your customer is a business with a procurement process, SEO first and AIO alongside it. Most people should pick one and do it properly rather than three badly.

What does this cost?

Google Ads management is $1,500 a month plus your ad spend. SEO consulting is $1,200 a month. AIO consulting is $1,800 one time. No setup fees and no long contract.
