What is actually different about marketing in Windsor

Windsor is separated from a metropolitan area of several million people by a river. That shapes everything. Search results here mix Canadian and American businesses, your ads can drift across the border and burn budget on people who will never cross it, and a meaningful share of local buying is influenced by what is available on the other side at what exchange rate. Getting the geographic targeting and the exclusions right is not a refinement in a Windsor account, it is the foundation.

The local economy is built on automotive manufacturing and the supply chain around it, which gives Windsor a B2B market with a very specific shape: relatively few buyers, long relationships, and purchasing decisions that do not come from a search ad. If you sell into that chain, what search does for you is make you credible and findable when a buyer or an engineer is scoping suppliers, which is SEO and AIO work rather than paid.

Alongside that sits an ordinary consumer economy, plus greenhouse agriculture across Essex County that is one of the largest of its kind in North America. Those are three different advertising problems, and the first useful conversation is about which of them you are in.

Questions Windsor businesses ask

Do you have an office in Windsor?

No. I am based in Toronto and work from home, so there is no Windsor office and I am not going to imply there is. Everything is run remotely over video and email, which is how I run every account.

Should we advertise into Michigan?

Only if you can actually serve customers there and have thought through the currency, the shipping and the border. If you can, it should be a separate campaign in its own right so the numbers stay readable. If you cannot, the more valuable move is excluding it properly, because default targeting will spend your budget there whether you meant it to or not.

We supply the automotive sector. Will Google Ads help?

Usually not much directly. Those relationships are long and the buyers are few, so there is very little search volume to capture. Where search earns its keep for a Windsor supplier is being findable and credible when an engineer or a purchasing manager is scoping options, which is SEO and AIO work. I would rather tell you that than sell you a campaign that cannot work.

Which service should a Windsor business start with?

If you sell to households with urgent needs, Google Ads once the map is right. If you sell into the manufacturing supply chain, SEO and AIO. Pick one and do it properly rather than three badly.

What does this cost?

Google Ads management is $1,500 a month plus your ad spend. SEO consulting is $1,200 a month. AIO consulting is $1,800 one time. No setup fees and no long contract.
