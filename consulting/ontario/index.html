What is actually different about marketing across Ontario

The most expensive setting in Ontario advertising is the province-wide radius. A campaign set to all of Ontario pays Toronto prices to reach customers in Sudbury, because the auction is priced by the most competitive part of the geography it covers. If you sell in more than one city here, the accounts almost always need to be split by market before anything else is worth doing.

The economics genuinely differ between those markets. A professional firm competing in the Greater Toronto Area is bidding against national brands with real budgets. The same firm competing in Kingston or Windsor is bidding against a handful of local competitors. Same service, same ad copy, completely different plan, and copying the Toronto playbook into a smaller Ontario market is how good businesses conclude that search does not work for them.

For agency owners and consultants specifically, there is a second problem. Your customers are not in one place and they are not searching for your city. They are searching for the service, sometimes with a city attached and sometimes not, and often from inside a chatbot now rather than a search box. That mix is different from what a local trades business faces, and it changes which of the three services below is worth your money first.

Questions Ontario businesses ask

Do you have an office in Ontario?

I live in Toronto and work from home, so yes I am in the province, but there is no office and no branch anywhere else in it. Everything is run remotely over video and email, which is how I run every account.

We sell across Ontario. Should we run one campaign or several?

Almost always several. One province-wide campaign lets the most competitive market in your geography set the price for all of them, which means you overpay in the smaller cities and underspend in the ones that matter. Splitting by market is usually the single biggest improvement available on a multi-city Ontario account.

I run an agency. Do you work with agencies or compete with them?

Both happen and I am straightforward about which. Some Ontario agencies bring me in on Google Ads or AIO work they do not run in-house, white labelled or not depending on what you prefer. Some agency owners hire me for their own marketing. If you are a competitor in the same lane, I will say so rather than take the call under false pretences.

Which of the three should an Ontario business start with?

It depends on the market you are in more than the industry you are in. In the Greater Toronto Area, paid search buys you attention while SEO takes a long time and costs real money. Outside it, SEO is usually the better first investment. AIO is worth doing almost everywhere right now because so few competitors have.

What does this cost?

Google Ads management is $1,500 a month plus your ad spend. SEO consulting is $1,200 a month. AIO consulting is $1,800 one time. No setup fees and no long contract, anywhere in the province.
