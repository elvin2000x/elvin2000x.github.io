What marketing actually costs a business in Toronto

Toronto is the largest market in the country, and that cuts both ways. There are more customers searching for what you sell here than anywhere else in Canada, and there are also more businesses bidding for those same clicks than anywhere else in Canada. A budget that would dominate a smaller Ontario market buys you a modest share of impressions in Toronto, and the difference between a campaign that pays for itself and one that quietly bleeds is usually structural rather than clever.

The practical consequence for most Toronto businesses is that you cannot win by outspending. You win by being more precise: tighter match types, a real negative keyword list, conversion tracking that counts a booked job rather than a form view, and landing pages that answer the specific thing the searcher typed. That is unglamorous work and it is most of the job.

It is also the market where you are most likely to already have an agency. If yours is doing well by you, keep them. I am useful when you want one accountable person who will show you the account, explain what is in it, and tell you plainly when a channel is not worth the money in financial services, professional services, technology, real estate and health care or anywhere else.

Questions Toronto businesses ask

No, and I would rather say so than imply otherwise. I live and work in Toronto but I work from home, so there is no address to visit. I can meet you in person for a coffee anywhere in the city, and the actual work happens remotely over video and email, which is how I run every account.

Which service should a Toronto business start with?

If you need revenue this quarter, Google Ads, because it is the only one that produces customers in weeks rather than months. If you can wait two or three quarters, SEO compounds and eventually costs you nothing per click. AIO is the cheapest of the three and the one your Toronto competitors are least likely to have touched. Most people should pick one and do it properly rather than three badly.

Is Toronto more expensive to advertise in than other Canadian cities?

Generally yes, because you are bidding against more competitors for the same searches, and in financial services, professional services, technology, real estate and health care the competition is heaviest. That does not make it a bad market, it makes precision worth more here than anywhere else. I would rather run a tight $3,000 a month in Toronto than a sloppy $10,000.

Do you work with businesses outside Toronto?

Yes. I work with clients across Canada and everything is run remotely, so where you are does not change the work or the price. Toronto is simply where I happen to live.
