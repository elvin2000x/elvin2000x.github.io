# The 90-Day Team Rollout Calendar

*The Systematic Advantage Toolkit*

Chapter 15's adoption plan, laid out week by week so you can run it rather than remember it. Copy this into your own calendar or project tool, put a real date on each week, and assign the AI Champion role before Week 1. The Champion runs the cadence and succeeds by making themselves unnecessary for the basics by Day 90.

Fill the `[brackets]`. Delete the rows that do not fit your team. The point is a consistent cadence rather than a perfect one.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1 to 30)

Goal: a shared language and one shared win, so the team stops treating AI as a private hobby.

| Week | Do this | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Name the AI Champion. Hold a 30-minute kickoff: why now, what we are trying, what we are not. Agree on the one workflow we will tackle first: `[the workflow]`. | A written charter and a named owner |
| **Week 2** | Run a vocabulary session. Agree on what our key terms mean so outputs do not drift. Start the team vocabulary doc. | A shared vocabulary document |
| **Week 3** | The Champion demos the first shared workflow live, end to end, including where it goes wrong. Team tries it on real work. | One workflow the whole team has seen run |
| **Week 4** | Collect what broke and what worked. Write the first shared workflow SOP for `[the workflow]`. | The team's first Workflow SOP |

## Phase 2: Expansion (Days 31 to 60)

Goal: move from one shared win to a small library, and from the Champion doing it to the team doing it.

| Week | Do this | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 5** | Start the team prompt library. Every member contributes their two best prompts, each with a note on the missing context it needs. | A seeded shared prompt library |
| **Week 6** | Add a second and third workflow SOP from real team needs: `[workflow 2]`, `[workflow 3]`. | Two more Workflow SOPs |
| **Week 7** | Peer practice session. Members pair up and run each other's workflows, surfacing the tacit steps that never made it into the SOP. | Revised, gap-filled SOPs |
| **Week 8** | Set common system instructions for shared tools, so the team starts from the same standing context. | A shared system-instruction baseline |

## Phase 3: Integration (Days 61 to 90)

Goal: this is just how the team works now, and it survives the Champion stepping back.

| Week | Do this | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 9** | Fold the workflows into normal process. Update the actual process docs so the AI steps are the documented way rather than a side experiment. | Updated standard operating process |
| **Week 10** | Set the review cadence: who keeps the prompt library and SOPs current, and how often. | A named maintenance owner and cadence |
| **Week 11** | The Champion steps back from the basics. Hand day-to-day ownership to the team. Champion moves to governance and the harder cases. | The team running without the Champion |
| **Week 12** | Retrospective. What compounded, what stalled, what to build next quarter. Schedule the first Quarterly Practice Audit for the team. | A next-quarter plan and a scheduled audit |

## The three things that make or break it

1. **Shared language before shared tools.** Skip the vocabulary session and every later output drifts. It feels slow and it is the fastest thing you will do.
2. **The Champion makes themselves unnecessary.** If the team still routes everything through one person at Day 90, the rollout failed even if the tools work.
3. **A cadence rather than a launch.** Adoption is thirteen ordinary weeks of steady work, and the calendar is the point.
