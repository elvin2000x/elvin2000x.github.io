# The Systematic Advantage Toolkit

*The companion kit to* The Systematic Advantage.

Welcome. This is the maintained, working half of the book. The book holds the durable principles, the ones that do not expire when a model updates. This kit holds the parts that only work as living files: the done-for-you starters, the dated specifics the book deliberately leaves out, and the templates you actually run. Use it alongside the book.

## What is in here

1. **The Worksheets, editable**: all eleven from Appendix D, the Plateau Diagnosis included, in reusable form so you run them per project instead of once in the book. *(all chapters)*
2. **System Instruction Starter Pack**: six complete, fill-in system instructions (marketer, analyst, consultant, manager, founder, writer). The book teaches you to write one. This hands you finished ones to start from. *(Chapter 6)*
3. **Tool-Selection Cheat Sheet**: which category of tool for which job, with the current product names, dated. The perishable list the book keeps out of its permanent pages. *(Chapter 4)*
4. **Model and Effort Quick-Reference**: how to match model tier and reasoning effort to the stakes, and where to find current names and prices yourself. Dated. *(Chapters 2 and 4)*
5. **The 90-Day Team Rollout Calendar**: Chapter 15's adoption plan as a week-by-week schedule you can drop into your calendar. *(Chapter 15)*
6. **The 20-Minute Quickstart**: the fastest path from zero to a working operating system, so you feel the difference today. *(Part 1)*
7. **The Quarterly Practice Audit Tracker**: a spreadsheet that scores your five practice dimensions over eight quarters so drift becomes visible, plus a how-to. *(Chapter 16)*
8. **The Collected Idea Bank**: every "you could do this" application from the book, gathered into one horizon-opening file. *(all chapters)*
9. **The Professional Prompt Library, importable**: the full library in a plain-text form you drop into your own prompt manager. *(Chapters 5 to 14)*

## How to get the most from it

- **Start with the Quickstart and the System Instruction Pack.** Twenty minutes and one pasted file, and you already have the core of an operating system.
- **Bookmark the two dated pages.** The Tool-Selection Cheat Sheet and the Model reference are the ones that go stale. When they do, you will get the refreshed version on the list you joined to get this kit.
- **Run the Quarterly Audit on a cycle.** It is the habit that keeps the whole practice compounding.

## A note on the dated pages

Two documents here carry an "as of" date on purpose. Product names, model names, and prices move faster than anything can print, so those pages are snapshots to maintain rather than facts to memorize. If a date at the top of a page is more than a quarter old, check the specifics before you rely on them.
