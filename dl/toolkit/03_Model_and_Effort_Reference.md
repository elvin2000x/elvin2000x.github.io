# Model and Effort Quick-Reference

*The Systematic Advantage Toolkit*

> **As of: early 2026. Model names and prices change monthly; this is a dated snapshot to maintain rather than a permanent table.** It gives you the durable skill (matching model tier and effort to the task) and points you to where the current specifics live, rather than quoting prices that will be wrong by the time you read them.

## The effort dial, in practice

The mistake is defaulting to the most powerful setting for everything. The skill is matching the tier to the stakes, exactly as Chapter 4 describes. Most tools now offer at least three tiers under different names. Think in tiers rather than in brand names.

| Tier | What it is for | Reach for it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Fast tier** | High-volume, low-stakes work: formatting, simple rewrites, quick lookups, first-pass sorting | The cost of a small error is a quick fix, and you want speed |
| **Standard tier** | The daily workhorse: most drafting, analysis, and back-and-forth | You are doing normal professional work and want a strong default |
| **Frontier tier** | The hardest thinking: strategy, multi-step reasoning, high-stakes review, anything you will put your name on | An error is expensive, or the problem needs real reasoning depth |

Many tools also expose a **reasoning-effort control** (a "think longer" or extended-reasoning setting) on top of the model choice. Turn it up for genuinely hard problems and off for routine work. It trades time and cost for depth, so spend it where depth pays.

## Choosing the tier by stakes

Pair this with the AI Task Matrix from Chapter 2:
- **Low cost of error** plus **high volume** goes to the fast tier.
- **Moderate stakes** goes to the standard tier with a normal review.
- **High stakes** goes to the frontier tier, reasoning turned up, and the Director's Due Diligence from Chapter 14 on the way out.

## Where the current specifics live

Because names and prices move, do not memorize them. Learn where to look:
- Each provider's own pricing and model pages are the only source of truth for current cost per token and current model names. Check them directly when cost matters.
- The provider's model comparison page tells you which current model sits in which tier.
- Your own tool's model picker usually labels the tiers for you. When it does not, the provider's docs will.

## The one habit that outlasts every model

Run the Stale Check from Chapter 11 on any model or pricing claim before it enters your work, including this page. Two questions: how likely is this to be outdated, and how would I check. For model and pricing facts, the answer to the first is always "very," and the answer to the second is "the provider's live page."
