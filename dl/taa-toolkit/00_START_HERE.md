# The Artificial Advantage Toolkit

*The companion kit to* The Artificial Advantage *by Elvin M. Peters.*

Welcome. The book teaches you to direct AI. This kit is the working half: the parts that are easier to use as files than as printed pages. Everything in it comes straight from the book, except the Tool-Selection Cheat Sheet, which carries the current product names the book leaves out on purpose.

## What is in here

1. **The Exercises, editable.** Every exercise from the book, in order, with space to record what happened. *(Chapters 2 to 13)*
2. **The 30-Day Practice Plan.** Your first month, from the first real task to a daily habit, with a checklist. *(Chapters 1 and 14)*
3. **The 3-Month AI Practice Plan.** The full plan from Chapter 14, with a check-in at the end of each month. *(Chapter 14)*
4. **The Professional Prompt Library, importable.** All twenty-five prompts from Appendix E, tagged by task, as a text file and as a spreadsheet you can import. *(Appendix E)*
5. **The Role Library.** All twenty-five Role statements from Appendix G, plus the formula for writing your own. *(Appendix G)*
6. **The Director's Cheat Sheet.** CLEAR, RTC, the Diagnostic Ladder and the Two-Step Verification Method on a few printable pages. *(Appendix C and Chapter 11)*
7. **The Tool-Selection Cheat Sheet.** Which kind of tool fits which job, with today's product names. Dated. *(Chapters 7 to 9)*

## Where to start

- **This week:** open the 30-Day Practice Plan and do the first-week task on something real.
- **Keep beside you:** print the Director's Cheat Sheet. It is the whole system on a few pages.
- **When a prompt stalls:** paste a Role from the Role Library, or start from the closest prompt in the library and edit the brackets.

## A note on the dated page

The Tool-Selection Cheat Sheet carries an "as of" date on purpose. Product names and prices move faster than a book can print. Treat that page as a snapshot to check, and look at the date before you rely on a specific product.
