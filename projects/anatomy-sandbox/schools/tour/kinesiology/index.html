Kinesiology and fitness: see how a lift moves the body

About 5 minutes · 5 stops

Learning outcome: Students can name the plane of a movement, see which muscles an exercise trains, and read what the Stretch colour shows.

1. The squat, from the side

Bodyweight Squat

You see: Press Play rep, then Left, and the hips and knees bend and straighten in one side-on plane.

Fact: Flexion and extension happen in the sagittal plane, around a frontal axis. (source)

2. The lateral raise, from the front

Side Lateral Raise

You see: Press Play rep, then Front, and the arms lift out to the sides in a plane that faces you.

Fact: Abduction and adduction happen in the coronal (frontal) plane. (source)

3. Deep muscles with Main only

Back extension

You see: Main only starts on here, since the lower-back muscles sit deep and the ones on top have to peel away.

Fact: The multifidus helps stabilize the spine by supporting it during movement and holding posture. (source)

4. From a muscle to its exercises

Do: Press Clear, search Rectus abdominis, pick the muscle and read Exercises that train it on its card.

You see: The muscle card lists up to four exercises that train it and a link to every abdominals exercise in the library.

Fact: Each rectus abdominis is split into segments by three bands of collagen called tendinous intersections. (source)

5. Watch muscles change length

Do: Press Walk at the top, then Wander, and under Colour press Stretch.

You see: As the body walks, red means a muscle is shorter than when standing and blue means longer. The colour shows length change only, with no effort or force.

Fact: The gluteus medius stabilizes the pelvis as you walk, which stops the opposite side from dropping. (source)
