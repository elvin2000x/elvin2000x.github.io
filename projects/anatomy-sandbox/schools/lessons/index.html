Lesson packs

Three packs are included, each with 5 activities and a printable answer sheet: PSW body systems, fitness program muscle ID, and Grade 12 kinesiology (PSK4U B1.1 and B1.2). An instructor can run one in their next lab without building anything. The answer sheets arrive with your campus link.

PSW Body Systems

Personal Support Worker programs (public colleges, career colleges, school board continuing education). 85 minutes in total, or one activity per class.

  1. Bones, falls and fractures
  2. Sit-to-stand and safe transfers
  3. Heart, lungs and breathing
  4. Digestion, urine and elimination care
  5. Skin and pressure injuries
