PSK4U B1.1 and B1.2

For: Grade 12 Introductory Kinesiology (PSK4U), Ontario · 95 minutes in total, or one activity per class

Activities 1 to 3 cover B1.1 (position, terms, planes and axes). Activities 4 and 5 cover B1.2 (bones, then origin, insertion and how muscles work together). Activity 5 follows the curriculum's own teacher prompt on the quadriceps and the biceps.

Curriculum fit. The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 9 to 12: Health and Physical Education (2015), PSK4U strand B, pages 180 to 181. B1.1: "use correct anatomical terminology to describe human movement (e.g., anatomical position, body planes and axes, basic movement terms such as flexion and extension, terms pertaining to body position such as anterior and superior)". B1.2: "identify the major muscles and bones of the musculoskeletal system (e.g., according to their location, origin and insertion, structure, function), and describe the ways in which they interact to create movement (e.g., flexion, extension, adduction, abduction)".

Source: Ontario Curriculum, Grades 9 to 12: Health and Physical Education (2015), PSK4U, pp. 180 to 181

Before class

  1. Open https://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/ on a classroom computer once, so you know how long it takes to load on your network. It opens on the skeleton.
  2. Click through each activity link. Each one opens the app in the right place.
  3. Print the student sheets (this page, from the Print button) and keep the answer sheet (psk4u-b1-answers.html) for yourself.

Activities

  1. Anatomical position and directional terms (B1.1, 15 min)
  2. Body planes with the Cut tool (B1.1, 15 min)
  3. Planes and axes of movement (B1.1, 20 min)
  4. The major bones (B1.2, 20 min)
  5. Origin, insertion and muscles working together (B1.2, 25 min)

Controls students will use

  1. Drag to turn the body, scroll or pinch to zoom, click any part to see its name.
  2. The search box ("Search structures") finds any part by name and flies to it.
  3. The Body tab holds Body systems (turn layers on and off), Colour and Cut.
  4. The Front, Back, Left, Right and Top buttons set the view. On an exercise, Play rep runs the movement.

Activity 1. Anatomical position and directional terms

B1.1 · about 15 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Open the apphttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/

Do

  1. Open the link and press Front.
  2. In Body systems, turn on Skin and Muscles so you can see what sits on top of what.
  3. Search and pick: Body of sternum, Vertebra L3, Radius, Ulna, Femur, Tibia.
  4. Press Front again and look at the hands. Which way do the palms face?

Answer

  1. Describe the anatomical position.
  2. Fill in the term: the sternum is ______ to the vertebral column.
  3. Fill in the term: in the anatomical position, the radius is ______ to the ulna.
  4. Fill in the term: the knee is ______ to the ankle.
  5. Fill in the term: the skin is ______ to the muscles.

Activity 2. Body planes with the Cut tool

B1.1 · about 15 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Open the apphttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/

Do

  1. Open the link. In Body systems, turn on Muscles, Bones and Organs.
  2. Under Cut, press Sagittal, then Coronal, then Transverse. Move the slider each time to sweep the cut through the body.

Answer

  1. Which cut divides the body into left and right parts?
  2. Which cut divides the body into front and back parts? Give its other name.
  3. Which cut divides the body into upper and lower parts?

Activity 3. Planes and axes of movement

B1.1 · about 20 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Bodyweight Squathttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Bodyweight_Squat
  • Side Lateral Raisehttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Side_Lateral_Raise

Do

  1. Open the Bodyweight Squat link and press Play rep. Press Left to watch it from the side.
  2. Open the Side Lateral Raise link and press Play rep. Press Front to watch it from the front.

Answer

  1. Squat, going down: name the action at the hip and at the knee, the plane and the axis.
  2. Lateral raise, going up: name the action at the shoulder, the plane and the axis.
  3. Give one movement that happens in the transverse plane, and its axis.

Activity 4. The major bones

B1.2 · about 20 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Open the skeletonhttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/

Do

  1. Open the link. The app starts on the skeleton.
  2. Search and pick each bone in the table on the answer sheet. Label where it sits and sort it by shape.

Answer

  1. Classify each bone by shape (long, short, flat, irregular or sesamoid): Femur, Humerus, Capitate bone, Calcaneus, Body of sternum, Scapula, Vertebra L3, Patella.
  2. Name the bone of the arm and the two bones of the forearm.
  3. Name the two bones of the leg below the knee. Which one carries most of the body's weight?

Activity 5. Origin, insertion and muscles working together

B1.2 · about 25 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Dumbbell Bicep Curlhttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Dumbbell_Bicep_Curl
  • Leg Extensionshttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Leg_Extensions
  • Barbell Squathttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Barbell_Squat

Do

  1. Open the Dumbbell Bicep Curl link. In Body systems, turn on Muscle attachments, then under Colour press Natural. The legend now shows origins in orange-red and insertions in blue.
  2. Click the biceps and follow each head to its attachments on the scapula and the radius.
  3. Open the Leg Extensions link and follow the four quadriceps muscles to their attachments.
  4. Open the Barbell Squat link. Under Colour, press Stretch, then press Play rep. Red means a muscle is shorter than when standing; blue means longer.

Teacher note. The Stretch colours show how much each muscle changes length, worked out from the joint angles. They do not show effort or force.

Answer

  1. Biceps brachii: give the origin of each head, the insertion and two actions.
  2. Quadriceps: where does rectus femoris start, where do the three vasti start, and where do all four insert? Which joints do they move?
  3. For knee extension, name the agonist and the antagonist.
  4. Squat with Stretch colour on: what colour are the vasti at the bottom, and what does that tell you?
  5. The quadriceps lengthen under load while you lower into the squat, and shorten as you stand up. Name each type of contraction.
