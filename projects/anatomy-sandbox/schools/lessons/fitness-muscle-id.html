Fitness Program Muscle ID

For: Fitness and Health Promotion diplomas, personal trainer courses, kinesiology labs · 75 minutes in total, or one activity per class

Five activities built on five staple lifts. Each link opens the exercise in the app: the main muscles light up strong red, the helpers soft orange, and the camera flies to them. Students name what they see, then connect each muscle to the joint action it makes.

Curriculum fit. Fitness and Health Promotion Program Standard (May 2020), Vocational Learning Outcome 2: "Develop, implement and evaluate safe training programs grounded in fundamentals of anatomy, bio-mechanics, cardiorespiratory physiology, and nutrition." Element d: programs "based in the fundamentals of cardiorespiratory physiology, anatomy, the muscoskeletal system, biomechanics, and exercise physiology."

Source: Fitness and Health Promotion Program Standard (May 2020), VLO 2, p. 8

Before class

  1. Open https://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/ on a classroom computer once, so you know how long it takes to load on your network. It opens on the skeleton.
  2. Click through each activity link. Each one opens the app in the right place.
  3. Print the student sheets (this page, from the Print button) and keep the answer sheet (fitness-muscle-id-answers.html) for yourself.

Activities

  1. The squat (Lower body push, 15 min)
  2. The Romanian deadlift (Hip hinge, 15 min)
  3. The push-up (Upper body push, 15 min)
  4. The lat pulldown (Upper body pull, 15 min)
  5. Calf raise and lateral raise (Isolation lifts, 15 min)

Controls students will use

  1. Drag to turn the body, scroll or pinch to zoom, click any part to see its name.
  2. The search box ("Search structures") finds any part by name and flies to it.
  3. The Body tab holds Body systems (turn layers on and off), Colour and Cut.
  4. The Front, Back, Left, Right and Top buttons set the view. On an exercise, Play rep runs the movement.

Activity 1. The squat

Lower body push · about 15 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Barbell Squathttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Barbell_Squat

Do

  1. Open the link and press Play rep.
  2. Turn on Main only to hide everything except the main muscles, then turn it off again.
  3. Click each lit muscle on the front of the thigh to read its name.

Answer

  1. Which muscle group does the app mark as the main mover? Name its four muscles.
  2. Which one of the four crosses the hip, and what does it do there?
  3. Which muscle extends the hip on the way up?
  4. Name the joint actions at the hip and the knee on the way up.

Activity 2. The Romanian deadlift

Hip hinge · about 15 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Romanian Deadlifthttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Romanian_Deadlift

Do

  1. Open the link and press Play rep.
  2. Press Back to view the back of the thigh. Click each lit muscle to read its name.

Answer

  1. Name the hamstring muscles.
  2. Which part of the hamstrings does not cross the hip?
  3. The hamstrings flex the knee and extend the hip. Which of those two actions does this lift train as you stand up?
  4. Which groups does the app list as helpers for this lift?

Activity 3. The push-up

Upper body push · about 15 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Pushupshttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Pushups

Do

  1. Open the link and press Play rep.
  2. Click the chest, the front of the shoulder and the back of the upper arm to read each name.

Answer

  1. Which muscle does the app mark as the main mover, and what are its two heads?
  2. Which muscle straightens the elbow? Name its three heads.
  3. Which part of the deltoid helps in a push-up, and what does it do at the shoulder?
  4. What is the elbow action on the way up?

Activity 4. The lat pulldown

Upper body pull · about 15 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Wide-Grip Lat Pulldownhttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Wide-Grip_Lat_Pulldown

Do

  1. Open the link. Press Back to see the muscles from behind.
  2. Click the large lit muscle across the side of the back, then the small one next to it near the shoulder blade.

Answer

  1. Which muscle does the app mark as the main mover? Which smaller muscle helps it?
  2. Name the three actions latissimus dorsi makes at the shoulder.
  3. Which two muscles bend the elbow as you pull the bar down?
  4. Which groups does the app list as helpers for this lift?

Activity 5. Calf raise and lateral raise

Isolation lifts · about 15 minutes · Name: ____________________

Open

  • Standing Calf Raiseshttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Standing_Calf_Raises
  • Seated Calf Raisehttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Seated_Calf_Raise
  • Side Lateral Raisehttps://elvinpeters.com/projects/anatomy-sandbox/#ex=Side_Lateral_Raise

Do

  1. Open the Standing Calf Raises link and press Back. Click each lit muscle on the calf.
  2. Open the Seated Calf Raise link and compare.
  3. Open the Side Lateral Raise link and press Play rep.

Answer

  1. Name the two calf muscles and the tendon they share.
  2. Which calf muscle crosses the knee, and which does not?
  3. What ankle action does a calf raise train?
  4. In the lateral raise, which part of the deltoid works hardest and what is the shoulder action?
