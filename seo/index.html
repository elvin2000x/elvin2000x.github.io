02

Why most small business SEO goes nowhere

Most small businesses spend money on SEO for months and never see it move a number that matters. Technical problems sit unfixed because nobody looks past the content calendar. Pages get written for keywords with no real buyer intent behind them. Structured data is missing or wrong, so search engines cannot tell what the page is even about. And the monthly report is full of impressions and rankings that never once get tied back to real traffic or real customers.

  • Technical issues quietly capping how much of the site gets indexed at all
  • Content built around keywords nobody with money to spend is searching
  • Missing or broken structured data, so search engines misread the page
  • Reports built to look busy instead of showing real progress
