Quick Reference Card

From The Systematic Advantage

One page to screenshot and keep beside your keyboard. Everything here is built in full inside the book.

CRAFT, the operating cycle

Configure the environment · Refine the brief · Apply through directed sessions · Forward to team and verification · Trust, calibrated to the stakes.

Volume 1 taught you to direct a session (CLEAR). Volume 2 builds the system so good sessions happen without depending on you to remember how.

The Professional Brief, five parts (Ch 5)

Write the brief rather than the prompt. 1. Objective: the one outcome. 2. Context: what the model cannot infer. 3. Constraints, ranked: load-bearing first, cosmetic last. 4. Output format: the shape you need back. 5. Success criteria: how you will know it is good.

Specialized versions: Audience-First (add recipient, relationship, tone, Voice Anchors) · Analysis (add the decision question and the input set) · Strategy (add the Assumption Register) · Leadership (add stakes and development intent).

The AI Task Matrix, what to delegate (Ch 2)

Score two axes: what the task is and what an error would cost. - Low cost → delegate freely, light read. - Moderate cost → delegate, verify the load-bearing parts. - High cost, or any factual claim with consequences → Director's Due Diligence.

Reasoning prompts, pick by risk (Ch 7)

  • Steelman: argue the strongest case against your own recommendation.
  • Pre-Mortem: assume it failed; name the mechanisms.
  • Assumption Audit: list what the plan needs to be true, rate each.
  • Expert Panel: multiple perspectives surface their disagreements.
  • Red-Team Read: the model attacks your deliverable before your toughest stakeholder does.

The Loop, a directed agentic session (Ch 9)

Blindspot Pass → Interview Me → Anchor with References → Prototype Wide → Log the Deviations → Quiz Me. Run it until it is reflex.

Director's Due Diligence, high stakes (Ch 14)

Evidence tiers: Tier 1 facts (check against a source) · Tier 2 reasoning (trace the logic) · Tier 3 synthesis (weigh against judgment). Match effort to tier; a full review lands in fifteen to twenty minutes.

Reputational Audit, the six pre-send questions: Tier 1 confirmed · Tier 2 traced · Tier 3 judged · second eyes if high-stakes · relationship or reputation risk considered · am I prepared to put my name on it.

Second-Opinion Run: same brief, second model. Agreement raises confidence; disagreement points at the claim to verify.

The five practice dimensions, audited quarterly (Ch 16)

Briefing · Memory and infrastructure · Verification · Voice · Range. Score each 1 to 5, then spend the next ninety days on the two lowest.

The memory ladder, six rungs (Ch 4)

Naked chat → saved prompts → standing instructions → context files → working folder → self-maintaining system. Move a piece of context up a rung the third time it recurs.
