From The Systematic Advantage

In Volume 2 the brief is the asset; the prompt you write from it is disposable. Build the brief once, here, then reuse it for every prompt on this piece of work. It saves in your browser as you type.

Start from an example:
The one outcome. What does a win look like for this piece of work?
What the model cannot infer: audience, background, the history behind this.
Load-bearing first (violating it makes the output unusable), then nice-to-have.
Exactly how you want it delivered.
How you will know it is good before it ships.
Your brief
Fill in the fields and your brief assembles here, ready to copy.
