About Elvin

Elvin Peters is a Toronto AI consultant, author, and builder, best described as a marketer who taught himself to build with AI. He spent a decade in marketing, half of it in performance, personally managing more than $50 million in profitable ad spend and running $1.5 million a month across two regulated brands for three years at a large publicly traded Canadian lender, with every campaign clearing compliance, legal, and creative review before launch.

He then advised the media teams at Thomson Reuters, TextNow, FindLaw, and Internet Brands inside Google's Large Customer Sales organization. Today he writes and runs a portfolio of AI systems he built and hosts himself, including fifteen niche directories, more than a dozen browser games, and his own email and publishing platform.

The Artificial Advantage, volume one of the AI Fluency Series, is published on Amazon in Kindle, paperback, and hardcover. Volumes two and three are in progress.

Programs

Stop Prompting, Start Directing

General audiences · mixed experience levels

The core method from the book. Most people treat AI like a search box and get search box results. Directing is a different habit, it takes about twenty minutes to teach, and the difference gets demonstrated live in front of the room rather than promised.

  • The directing method, shown live instead of described
  • A clear reason why their own results have been mediocre
  • One task from their week to rebuild before Friday

The Review Gate

Regulated industries · risk and leadership audiences

How to put AI into work that somebody checks. Built on three years coordinating compliance, legal, and creative review before every regulated campaign went live, and on AI systems that queue everything for a human before anything publishes.

  • Where human sign-off belongs and where it is theatre
  • What an AI use policy has to say to be followed
  • The questions a leader should be asking their own teams

What AI Costs and What It Produces

Leadership audiences · budget holders · skeptics

The ledgers from systems run on his own money, followed by the failures those same ledgers recorded. Both halves are the talk, and the failures are the half nobody else shows.

  • A way to estimate the real cost of an AI initiative
  • Why volume without a quality gate is worth nothing
  • How to tell a real AI result from a good demo

AI Inside a Marketing Team

Marketing conferences · agency and association events

Half a decade of performance marketing, including directing AI bidding and AI creative long before it reached the headlines. What worked, what quietly did not, and where the judgment still has to be human.

  • Where AI belongs in a media and content workflow, step by step
  • What to test, what to automate and what to leave alone
  • How to move faster while brand and compliance standards hold
