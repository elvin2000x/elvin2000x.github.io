What is AIO, in plain terms?

AIO, or AI answer engine optimization, is the practice of structuring a site so tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews can find your content, understand it correctly, and cite your business by name instead of a competitor's. It uses structured data, clear direct-answer content, and crawlable pages, the same techniques search engines already reward.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO earns rankings in a search results page. AIO earns a citation inside an AI-generated answer, which increasingly replaces the search results page entirely for a growing share of questions. The two overlap heavily but are not the same job, which is why they are offered as separate builds. Many businesses want both, and SEO consulting is its own service if that is the fit.

It depends on the size of the site, which is why we agree the timeline on the call before any work starts. You will know the plan and the handover date up front, so there are no surprises.
